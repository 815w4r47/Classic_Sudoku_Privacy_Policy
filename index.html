Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Sudoku app ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal details to use it.

All game data including statistics, preferences, and settings are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to us or any third party.

Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information to serve relevant ads, including:

  • Device advertising ID
  • General location (country/region)
  • Device type and operating system version
  • App usage interactions with ads

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. For more information on how Google uses data, please visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Storage

The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never sent to any server:

  • Game statistics (games played, win rate, best times)
  • App preferences (dark mode setting)

This data is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third Party Services

The app uses the following third party service:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements.
    Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at your registered developer email address on the Google Play Store.
